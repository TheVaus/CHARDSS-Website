# Chardss: Cards, Chess & Chaos

Chardss is chess with a shared deck of cards. Every card bends one rule of chess, and you pay for cards with the pieces you lose.

## What you need

- A standard chessboard and pieces
- The Chardss deck (40 cards)
- Some way to track each player's points (a notepad, tokens or a die)

## Setup

1. Set up the chessboard with all pieces in their standard starting positions.
2. Shuffle the shared deck of cards.
3. Each player draws 3 cards into their hand *(keep hands hidden from each other)*.

## Turn structure

On your turn, follow these steps **in this order**:

1. **Play a card** *(optional)*
   - You may play one card per turn.
   - To play a card, you must pay its point cost *(see [Points system](#points-system))*.
   - If you do not have enough points or do not wish to play a card, skip this step.
2. **Make your move**
   - Make a legal chess move following standard chess rules *(unless modified by the card you played)*.
3. **Draw back to 3 cards**
   - If you have fewer than 3 cards in hand, draw from the shared deck until you have 3 cards again.
   - If the deck is empty, you do not draw cards.

## Points system

You gain points whenever **your own** pieces are captured. You gain points equal to the value of the piece that was captured:

| Piece | {Pawn} Pawn | {Knight} Knight | {Bishop} Bishop | {Rook} Rook | {Queen} Queen |
| ----- | :---------: | :-------------: | :-------------: | :---------: | :-----------: |
| Points | 1 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 9 |

> **Note:** You do not gain points for capturing your opponent's pieces.

## Cards, deck and discard pile

**Playing a card**

- You must have the card in your hand and pay its point cost.
- After playing it, place the card in the shared discard pile.

**Drawing cards**

- Draw from the top of the shared deck until you have 3 cards in hand.
- If the deck is empty, you do not draw cards.
- When both players have no cards left, the game continues as regular chess.

## Golden rules

- All normal rules of chess remain in effect unless a card changes them.
- If a card's effect contradicts a standard chess rule, the card takes priority.
- Cards cannot be played if their effect would result in an illegal position *(for example, you cannot play a card to skip your move while your {King} is in check)*.

## End of the game

The game ends by the standard chess win conditions: checkmate, stalemate, resignation, draw by repetition, and so on.

## House rules

Feel free to take out cards you don't like or bend the rules to your liking. There is no right way to play — Chardss is all about customization.
